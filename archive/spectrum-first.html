The wavelength axis (100–3000 nm, logarithmic) is laid onto the audible range (20 Hz–20 kHz, logarithmic), long wavelength to low frequency, and the spectral power becomes the noise's power envelope. Colour is computed from the spectrum with the CIE 1931 colour matching functions, so it is an output, not an input. Space plays / stops when the editor has focus.
